Jollof Rice Recipe

This is for 4 people, a very friendly budget lol.

Ingredients

    Rice:

    • 3 cups Golden Sella basmati or long grain rice
    • Water to parboil

Stewbase:

  • 5 big fresh tomatoes
  • 3 big tatashe (red bell pepper)
  • 2 shombo (optional for heat)
  • 2 big onions (1 for blending,
    • 1 for slicing)
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil (not too much)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste (Gino or De Rica) — this gives red color
  • 1 tsp thyme1 tsp curry
  • 2 bay leaves1 tbsp Jollof seasoning (or Benny chicken if you don't have)
  • 3 seasoning cubes (Maggi)Salt to taste
  • 1 cup chicken/beef stock (or water if none)

For that party smoky taste:

  • Small piece of butter + foil paper (I’ll explain)

Instructions

  1. Make the stew.
    • Blend tomatoes, tatashe, shombo, and 1 onion. Don't add too much water.
    • Heat pot, add oil, slice remaining onion and fry for 1 min
    • Add tomato paste, fry for 3-4 mins until it turns dark red (very important, this removes sour taste)
    • Pour blended mix, add thyme, curry, bay leaves, seasoning cubes. Fry till water dries and oil floats on top — about 15-20 mins. Stir so it doesn't burn.
  2. Perboil rice:
    • Boil rice for 8 mins, wash with cold water, drain. This removes starch so it doesn't gum.
  3. Combine:
    • Add stock to stew, add salt, taste — it should be a bit salty because rice will absorb it
    • Pour parboiled rice inside, mix well. The stew should cover rice. If not, add small water — NOT plenty. Jollof needs to steam, not boil like white rice.
    • Cover with foil paper first, then pot cover on top. This traps steam.
    • Cook on LOW heat for 20 mins. DON'T open.
  4. The party trick:
    • After 20 mins, open, stir from bottom, taste. If still hard, sprinkle small water, cover again.
    • For smoky: Put small foil bowl inside pot on top of rice, add tiny butter inside foil, heat small piece of charcoal or use hot spoon to make butter smoke, cover pot for 2 mins. That smoky smell is party jollof.

Serve with fried plantain and chicken. Done.
